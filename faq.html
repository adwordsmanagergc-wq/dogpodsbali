Frequently Asked Questions

Everything Bali dog owners want to know before booking. Anything missing? Message Dharma on WhatsApp.

How much does a dog pod cost in Bali?▾

Pricing depends on your bike model and the size of pod your dog needs. Single-side pods from IDR 1,900,000; double-side pods from IDR 2,900,000. Tell Dharma your bike model and request on WhatsApp for an exact quote.

How long does it take to make?▾

Most pods are completed in 5 to 6 days maximum, including fitting to your bike in Bali.

Will it fit my bike?▾

Yes. Every pod is custom-fitted. Common bikes: Honda Vario, Scoopy, PCX, BeAT, Genio, ADV, Yamaha NMAX, Aerox, Fazzio, Mio, Vespa LX/Sprint/GTS, Kawasaki, Suzuki, Royal Enfield. Different bike? Send Dharma a photo.

Can I choose the colour?▾

Yes — any colour. The gel coat is UV-stable, so it holds up to tropical sun without fading or peeling.

Can you add a window?▾

Yes — clear acrylic windows on the front, sides or in a panoramic wraparound. Most riders pick at least a side window.

Is it safe for my dog?▾

Padded interior, dedicated ventilation, internal harness clip-in points and secure mounting bolted directly to your bike's rack — never strapped on.

What size dog does it fit?▾

Built to your dog's measurements — small, medium or large. Frenchies, mini Daschies, Bali heelers, Labs and bigger; Dharma sizes the pod around your dog, not the other way round.

Why fibreglass and not wood?▾

Bali's tropical climate is brutal on wood — humidity, monsoon rain and salt air swell, warp and rot plywood within months. Fibreglass is fully waterproof, mould-proof, UV-stable, lighter and stronger on impact. It's the same material used for boats in Benoa and surfboards in Uluwatu, because nothing else holds up here long-term.

How long does a fibreglass dog pod last?▾

A properly built fibreglass pod can easily last many years in Bali. The shell doesn't rot, the gel coat resists UV, and any small dings can be patched and re-coated like a surfboard.

How heavy is a fibreglass dog pod?▾

Significantly lighter than the equivalent in plywood. Exact weight depends on size and extras, but pods are light enough that a 125–160cc scooter handles normally.

Is the pod really waterproof in Bali rain?▾

Yes. The fibreglass shell is sealed and finished with a marine-grade gel coat — Bali downpours run straight off. Drainage holes deal with anything that gets in past the lid or window.

Can I take the pod off when I'm not riding with my dog?▾

Yes — ask for the optional quick-release mounting adapter and the pod comes on and off in seconds without tools.

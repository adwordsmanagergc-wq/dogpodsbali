What we fix on fibreglass dog pods

If your pod is built from fibreglass — ours or someone else's — most damage is repairable. Same techniques used on boats and surfboards.

🩹

Cracks & impact damage

Sanded back, re-laminated with fresh glass cloth and resin, then re-coated and matched to your existing colour.

✨

Gel-coat scratches & UV fade

Cut, polish or full re-coat depending on the depth. We can refresh a sun-faded pod back to factory finish.

🛏️

Padding & interior

Replace worn or chewed padding, refresh fabric covers, deep-clean the inside.

🔗

Harness anchors & clips

Re-bonded or replaced safely so your dog stays clipped in.

Acrylic windows

🌬️

Ventilation upgrades

🎨

Re-colour / re-finish

Tired of your pod's colour? Full re-coat in any colour you want, matt or gloss.

Honest answer about wood pods

If your dog pod is built from wood — we won't repair it.

It's not that we can't. It's that we won't take your money for a fix that we already know will fail again within months.

Why wood pods keep failing in Bali

  • ❌ Once plywood absorbs moisture, the layers delaminate from the inside — you can't seal it back up from the outside
  • ❌ Mould spores live in the timber and re-bloom every wet season
  • ❌ Screws and joints corrode faster than you can replace them
  • ❌ Salt air on the coast (Canggu, Uluwatu, Sanur) accelerates everything
  • ❌ Painting over rot just hides it — the wood underneath keeps breaking down

What we recommend instead

A fresh build in hand-laid fibreglass, sized exactly to your dog and fitted to your existing bike rack. Same shape, same colour if you want, but built from a material that survives Bali long-term.

  • ✅ Fully waterproof, mould-proof, sealed shell
  • ✅ Lighter than ply — better handling on the bike
  • ✅ UV-stable gel coat — no peeling paint
  • ✅ Lasts many years, not seasons
  • ✅ Repairable like a surfboard if it ever takes a knock

Repair FAQ

Do you repair dog pods in Bali?▾

Yes — we repair fibreglass dog pods and dog carriers built by us or by other makers. Common repairs: cracks, dings, gel-coat damage, padding, harness anchors, drainage, re-fit to a new bike.

Do you repair wooden dog pods?▾

No. Bali's tropical climate continues to rot the timber from the inside out, so any patch we do will fail again within months. We don't take money for fixes we know won't last. The right answer is a fresh build in fibreglass — happy to quote that for you.

Why won't you fix my plywood dog pod?▾

Once plywood has absorbed moisture and started to delaminate, swell or grow mould, surface repairs are cosmetic only. The wood underneath keeps breaking down. It's an honesty thing — we'd rather not take your money for that.

How much does a dog pod repair cost?▾

Depends entirely on the damage. Small chips and gel-coat scratches can be done for a fraction of a new pod. Larger structural work or a full re-finish costs more. Send Dharma photos on WhatsApp for an estimate.

How long does a repair take?▾

Most fibreglass repairs are 2–4 days. Full rebuilds are 4–7 days on average — same as a new build.

Do you repair pods built by other Bali makers?▾

If it's fibreglass — yes. If it's wood or plywood (regardless of who built it) — no, for the reasons above.

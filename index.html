About Dharma's Dog Pods

No middlemen. No templates. You message Dharma on WhatsApp, he asks the right questions, and 5–6 days later you and your dog are riding around Bali together.

Built different

Fibreglass, not wood — built to beat Bali weather

Most dog pod builders in Bali still use plywood or timber. It looks fine in the workshop. Six months of monsoon rain, salty Canggu air and 95% humidity later, it doesn't. Wood absorbs water, swells, warps, splits, grows mould and eats screws alive. We've seen it dozens of times.

That's why every Dharma's Dog Pod is hand-laid in fibreglass — the same material used for boat hulls in Bali's harbours, surfboards in Uluwatu, and the bodywork on your scooter itself. If it survives the ocean and the Bali sun, it'll survive the school run.

How we build in fibreglass
💧

100% waterproof

Fibreglass doesn't absorb water. Bali downpour? Beach spray? It runs straight off — no swelling, no soaking through to your dog.

🦠

No rot, no mould

Wood pods grow mould in tropical humidity within months. Fibreglass is inert — it won't rot, mildew or smell, even after years of wet seasons.

🪶

Lighter on your bike

Fibreglass is dramatically lighter than equivalent wood, so your scooter handles better, brakes shorter, and uses less fuel.

💪

Stronger on impact

Layered glass fibre flexes and absorbs hits instead of cracking like ply. The same reason it's used for boat hulls and surfboards.

☀️

UV stable

Gel-coat finish doesn't fade, crack or peel under tropical sun the way painted plywood does after one dry season.

🎨

Smooth, mouldable, any colour

Fibreglass is laid into a custom mould — meaning curved, aerodynamic shapes and a perfect glass-smooth finish that wood simply can't match.
